## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Meridian branding on social cards

The site SHALL generate and reference social card imagery that follows the Meridian brand and current canonical site URLs.

#### Scenario: A feature page is shared

- **WHEN** a feature page publishes its social metadata
- **THEN** the referenced generated card SHALL display the Meridian wordmark and correct Meridian site URL
- **AND** the generated card and its reference SHALL be verified in the site build
